# Google Play sources used for HariCart publishing guidance

Research checked on 2026-08-18.

## Official sources

- [Get started with Play Console](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6112435?hl=en): Google documents a US$25 one-time Play Console registration fee. Accepted cards listed are Mastercard, Visa, American Express, Discover (U.S. only), and Visa Electron (outside the U.S. only); prepaid cards are not accepted and availability may vary by location. The setup flow includes accepting the Developer Distribution Agreement, choosing an account type, verifying identity, and, for personal accounts, meeting testing and device-verification requirements.
- [App testing requirements for new personal developer accounts](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en): Personal accounts created after November 13, 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers opted in continuously for at least 14 days before applying for production access. Production access is requested from the Play Console Dashboard and includes questions about the app, testing, and production readiness.
- [Meet Google Play's target API level requirement](https://developer.android.com/google/play/requirements/target-sdk): Starting August 31, 2026, new apps and app updates must target Android 16 (API level 36) or higher for submission to Google Play. Existing apps must target Android 15 (API level 35) or higher to remain available to new users on newer Android versions; exceptions apply to certain form factors.
- [Provide information for Google Play's Data safety section](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en): All developers must complete the Data safety form and disclose how user data is collected, shared, and protected, including data handled by third-party SDKs. The form is completed under Play Console > App content > Data safety. Developers must answer collection/security questions, identify relevant data types and purposes, and state whether data is encrypted in transit and whether users can request deletion when applicable.
- [Prepare your app for review](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9859455?hl=en): Review preparation includes a privacy policy, ads declaration, sign-in details that enable reviewer access, target-audience/content declarations, permission declarations when applicable, and content ratings. HariCart is invite-only, so reviewer access instructions or a review account/test invitation must be supplied without exposing private resident data.
- [Understanding Google Play's Payments policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10281818?hl=en): Google Play Billing is for digital goods/services. The policy explicitly lists physical-goods purchases such as groceries and physical services such as food delivery as not supported by Google Play Billing. HariCart may therefore use its checkout/UPI/COD flow for grocery orders, while still complying with payment-provider and local legal requirements.
